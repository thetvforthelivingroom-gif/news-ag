Lawsuit says Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceXAI and Google made illegal agreement on AI slowdown

By AP News · Sep 19, 2026
<p><strong>A federal lawsuit filed in the Southern District of New York alleges that four leading AI companies—Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceXAI, and Google—colluded to slow the pace of artificial‑intelligence development.</strong> The complaint, brought by a coalition of former employees and industry observers, claims that the firms coordinated to restrict the release of new AI models, limit access to training data, and delay the deployment of advanced algorithms. The plaintiffs argue that these actions violate the Sherman Antitrust Act by creating a collusive framework that stifles competition and innovation in the AI sector. The case is the first formal legal challenge to alleged collusion among these firms and could reshape the industry’s regulatory landscape.</p> <h2>What happened</h2> <p>The lawsuit, filed in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York, seeks injunctive relief, damages, and an order to dismantle the alleged agreement. It also requests that the companies disclose internal communications related to the conduct. Whistleblower testimony cited in the complaint indicates that executives discussed strategies to slow AI development to avoid regulatory scrutiny and maintain a competitive edge. No company has issued a public statement in response to the allegations, and the case remains pending.
