The key is whether the conduct goes beyond legitimate cooperation and actually restrains trade.⁴</li> </ol> <p>To prove an antitrust violation, plaintiffs would need to show direct communications or agreements between the parties that indicate a coordinated effort to slow AI development, intent to restrain trade rather than legitimate business objectives, and actual or potential harm to consumers or competitors.
