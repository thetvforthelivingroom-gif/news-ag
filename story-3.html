With a range of up to 300 km, ATACMS can strike deep into enemy territory, beyond the reach of conventional artillery. The system’s precision guidance and high‑explosive warheads make it a potent tool for targeting command and control nodes, supply depots, and troop concentrations.</p> <h2>Official statements: receipt, not use</h2> <p>President Zelenskiy’s X post on 28 September 2023 was accompanied by a brief caption: “Today we received the ATACMS from the United States.
