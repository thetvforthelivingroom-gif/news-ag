Ukraine deploys FP-7 tactical ballistic missile in combat for first time, Zelenskiy says

By yahoo.com · Sep 19, 2026

While the deployment signals Ukraine’s growing technical ingenuity, analysts caution that the operational effectiveness and strategic impact remain uncertain amid a contested information environment.

A brief video clip showed a launch pad and a rocket ascending, but no clear evidence of impact or target confirmation was provided. The FP‑7 is a tactical ballistic missile originally designed by Russia for short‑to‑medium range strikes. Its 300‑kilometre reach allows Ukrainian forces to target deep‑behind‑enemy lines, including logistics hubs and command posts. However, the system’s reliability under combat conditions has yet to be fully tested, and the Ukrainian army is still training crews for operational deployment.
