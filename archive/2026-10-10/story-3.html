Likewise, the <em>Star Tribune</em> and FOX 9 Minneapolis‑St. Paul archives show no coverage of the alleged incident. The absence of any court docket, police report, or reputable news coverage indicates that the story was fabricated or the result of a misunderstanding of a separate, unrelated incident. We reached out to the Minnesota Attorney General’s Office for confirmation. The office confirmed that no case involving a vinegar assault on a public official has been filed in the state in the past decade.
