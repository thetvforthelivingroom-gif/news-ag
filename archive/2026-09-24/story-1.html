The impact caused a pile‑up that stalled traffic for several minutes. The collision forced a Los Angeles Police Department helicopter, which was en route to a medical emergency, to perform an emergency maneuver. The helicopter then crashed into a nearby office building, killing the pilot and several bystanders.</p> <p>Local outlets—including AP News, ABC11, NBC Los Angeles, and the Los Angeles Times—reported that the driver of the vehicle that struck the bus has been charged with murder for the helicopter crash [T2].
