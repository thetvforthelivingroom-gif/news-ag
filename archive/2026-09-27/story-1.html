The driver of the car, whose name has not yet been released, was arrested at the scene and subsequently charged with murder. The charge follows a preliminary investigation that suggests the driver’s collision may have contributed to the conditions that led to the helicopter’s loss of control. <span class="citation">[T3]</span></p> <p>The police report indicates that the driver was traveling at a speed that exceeded the posted limit for the area and failed to maintain a safe following distance. Witnesses noted that the vehicle’s brakes appeared to be engaged, but the driver was unable to prevent the collision. After the impact, the bus’s front end was severely damaged, compromising its structural integrity and possibly affecting the helicopter’s ability to navigate around it. The investigation is still underway, but the prosecution has argued that the driver’s negligence directly precipitated the chain of events that culminated in the fatal helicopter crash. <span class="citation">[T3]</span></p> <p>The murder charge is unprecedented in the context of a traffic incident. Traditionally, reckless driving that results in death is prosecuted as vehicular manslaughter or negligent homicide. However, prosecutors have cited the driver’s extreme disregard for human life and the foreseeability of the consequences as meeting the threshold for malice aforethought.
